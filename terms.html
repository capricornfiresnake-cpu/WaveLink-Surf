Last updated: August 2026

These terms apply when you book a lesson or rental with Wavelink Surf. By booking, you agree to the terms below.

Booking & payment

Private and semi-private lessons are booked and paid online through our scheduling and payment providers; your spot is confirmed once payment is received. Group lessons are booked by request — we'll confirm availability and send a secure payment link to reserve your session.

Cancellations & rescheduling

We ask for at least [24 hours'] notice to cancel or reschedule a lesson. Cancellations made with sufficient notice may be rescheduled or refunded; cancellations made with less notice, or no-shows, may not be eligible for a refund.

Weather & ocean conditions

Surfing depends on the ocean. If conditions are unsafe or unworkable, we may reschedule your lesson at no charge. Your safety, and our coach's judgment on the day, always come first.

Assumption of risk & safety

Surfing is a physical activity that carries inherent risks, including those related to waves, currents, marine life, equipment, and other people in the water. You participate voluntarily and are responsible for representing your health, swimming ability, and experience level honestly. Participants (or a parent/guardian for minors) may be required to sign a separate liability waiver before entering the water.
