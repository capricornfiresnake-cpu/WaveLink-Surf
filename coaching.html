Personal Pro Coaching

The pursuit of mastery

For surfers committed to maximizing their potential, Pro-Coaching delivers technical, detail-driven instruction at every level — the perfect tune-up before a contest, or the push you need to break through a plateau, regardless of age or ability.

Pro-Coaching is reserved exclusively for our premiere instructors — coaches who bring the highest level of expertise, motivation, and guidance to help you reach your goals faster than you thought possible.

Longboard to Shortboard

Built for the transition — and for going deeper.

Making the jump from longboard to shortboard? This program is built for exactly that transition, along with any surfer looking to sharpen their skills at a deeper level. Every session includes video analysis so you can see your technique the way your coach does, plus access to our quiver of demo boards spanning every skill level — so you're always riding the right board for where you're at.

What's Included

Technique first. Everything else follows.

i.

Video analysis

See your technique the way your coach does, frame by frame — the detail that makes progress stick.

ii.

Demo board quiver

Access to boards spanning every skill level, so you're always riding the right board for where you're at.

iii.

Land training

Strength, balance, and body mechanics built on the sand before you ever paddle out.

We put a heavy emphasis on technique, backed by land training designed to build strength, balance, and body mechanics before you ever paddle out. It's a steady, structured approach to developing real technique, confidence, and wave-reading ability — improvements that stick, and that translate directly into performance in the water.

Multi-Session Packages

Start with a single lesson. Build a strategy from there.

For surfers with extended training or competition goals, multi-session packages are available. We recommend starting with a single lesson so your instructor can assess your current level and build a personalized strategy from there.
