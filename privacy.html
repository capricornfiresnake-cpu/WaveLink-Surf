Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wavelink Surf ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website or book a lesson with us.

Information we collect

When you book a lesson, contact us, or request a group session, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, the lesson details you select, and any notes you share with us. When you pay online, your payment is processed by our payment provider — we do not see or store your full card details.

How we use your information

  • To schedule, confirm, and manage your surf lessons.
  • To contact you about your booking, availability, or conditions.
  • To process payments and send receipts.
  • To respond to your questions and improve our service.

Service providers

We use trusted third-party services to run our business, and information you provide may be processed by them for the purposes above:

  • Acuity Scheduling — online booking and calendar.
  • Stripe — secure payment processing.
  • Vercel — website hosting.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. We share only what is needed to provide our services and never sell your personal information.

Cookies

Our own pages do not set marketing or tracking cookies. Our booking and payment providers may set cookies that are necessary for their tools to work when you book or pay.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by contacting us. We will respond in line with applicable law.

Children's privacy

Lessons for minors are arranged by a parent or guardian, who provides the booking information on the minor's behalf.

Contact us
