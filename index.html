Our Lessons

Personalized lessons with elite instructors

Three ways to get in the water — each private in spirit, each built around your goals, your pace, and the swell in front of you.

Private Lesson

1.5 hours · 1 surfer · 1 instructor

$125 per surfer

All eyes on you. Focused, one-on-one coaching that gets you further, faster.

Semi-Private

1.5 hours · 2 surfers · 1 instructor

$98 per surfer

Share the session with a partner or friend and keep the coaching close and personal.

Group Lesson

Minimum 3 surfers · 1 coach per 2 surfers

$80 per surfer

Family and friends together in the water, with a low coach-to-surfer ratio throughout.

Why Wavelink

The difference is in the details

i.

Vetted, elite instructors

Every coach is hand-selected for expertise, judgment, and the patience to meet you exactly where you are.

ii.

Built around you

Your goals, your pace, your sticking points. No two lessons look alike — and neither do two surfers.

iii.

Reading the ocean

Positioning, timing, wave selection. We teach you to read the water so progress continues long after the lesson ends.

The Coastline

Some of the best waves you'll ever ride — and a coach who knows exactly where to find them.

From the reefs of La Jolla to the beach breaks of Encinitas and Carlsbad, we choose the spot to match the day and your ability. You bring a swimsuit, towel and sunscreen. We bring the board, the wetsuit, and years of local knowledge.
